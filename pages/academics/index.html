Academic Life

At TD Medical College, learning goes beyond textbooks. Our teaching methodology integrates classroom lectures with hands-on clinical training, ensuring students develop both knowledge and practical skills essential for medical practice.

The college is attached to a 1000-bed hospital that provides students with extensive clinical exposure across all medical and surgical specialties. From the first year itself, students are introduced to patient interaction, fostering empathy and clinical acumen.

Community outreach programmes form an integral part of the curriculum, with students regularly participating in rural health camps, awareness drives, and public health initiatives. This holistic approach ensures our graduates are not just skilled doctors but compassionate healthcare leaders.
