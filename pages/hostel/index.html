Hostel Overview

TD Medical College provides comfortable and well-maintained hostel accommodation for its students. With a total of 7 hostel blocks, the college ensures that every student has access to a safe and conducive living environment.

Separate hostels are available for men and women, each equipped with modern amenities and supervised by dedicated wardens. The hostels feature 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance, ensuring the safety and well-being of all residents.

Each hostel block is designed to foster a sense of community while providing the privacy and comfort students need for their academic pursuits. Common areas, study rooms, and recreation facilities are available in every block.
