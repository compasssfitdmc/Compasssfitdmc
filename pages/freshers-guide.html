FIRST STEPS

Getting Started

Follow these steps to begin your journey at TDMC

Complete Admission

Submit all required documents and complete the fee payment process at the admission office.

Move into Hostel

Collect your room key from the warden and settle into your new home.

Get Your ID Card

Visit the admin office with your photo to receive your student identity card.

Start Classes

Attend the orientation programme and begin your journey in medicine.
