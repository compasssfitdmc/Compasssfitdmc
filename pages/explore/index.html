About Alappuzha

Alappuzha, fondly known as the Venice of the East, is a breathtaking destination in the heart of Kerala. Famous for its intricate network of backwaters, lagoons, and canals, this coastal city offers a unique blend of natural beauty and cultural richness.

The city is renowned for its houseboat cruises through the tranquil backwaters, the spectacular Nehru Trophy Snake Boat Race, and its pristine beaches that stretch along the Arabian Sea coastline.

Alappuzha is also the hub of India's coir industry and boasts a rich maritime history as a historic port city that once traded spices with the world. Whether you're seeking spiritual solace at ancient temples, relaxation on golden beaches, or adventure on the backwaters, Alappuzha has something for everyone.
